Start AIDJ first

The mixing happens on your computer

This page is the remote control. Downloading, analysing and mixing the songs is done by the AIDJ app on your own computer, and it isn't running yet.

  1. Download AIDJ and unzip it.
  2. Windows: double-click start.bat. macOS / Linux: follow the steps in the README.
  3. Keep its window open, then press Check again. If your browser asks whether this site may access apps on your device, allow it.
